# 写字本 xiezi

A Chinese-learning app built from textbooks: each book has lessons, and each lesson is practised through skills and exercises until it is learnt.

## Lessons and practice

**Lesson**:
One numbered unit of a book: its words, example sentences, texts, grammar points and workbook tasks.
_Avoid_: Unit, chapter

**Skill**:
One of a lesson's six areas of practice: Write, Listen, Speak, Read, Words, Exercises.
_Avoid_: Tab, section, category

**Exercise**:
One way to practise inside a skill, such as Dictation, Tones or Build the sentence.
_Avoid_: Mode, quiz, game, activity

**Item**:
One thing an exercise asks about: a word, a character, a sentence, a text or a workbook task.
_Avoid_: Question, card, entry

**Part**:
The items that one or more exercises share and that count once toward lesson %; Flashcards, Match pairs and Word quiz are one part.
_Avoid_: Group, set

**Round**:
The items an exercise asks in one go, the ones not yet done first.
_Avoid_: Session, set, batch

## Progress

**Done**:
An item is done once it has been answered right, or put right after a mistake; written characters and dictation words are also done after five tries.
_Avoid_: Mastered, learnt, complete

**Lesson %**:
How much of a lesson is done: the average, over its skills, of the share of items done, counting public lesson content only.
_Avoid_: Score, progress, completion rate

**Open**:
An item of a lesson that is not done yet.
_Avoid_: Left, remaining, pending

## Tests

**Test**:
A timed, scored paper on the book page, taken in one sitting: a small test (小测) every three lessons, a mid-book test (期中) at each half, and a final test (期末) for the whole book.
_Avoid_: Exam, quiz

**Paper**:
The questions picked for one attempt of a test, built from lesson content.
_Avoid_: Question set, test sheet

**Test part**:
One of a paper's five sections: 听力, 词汇, 语法, 阅读, 书写. Not a lesson Part.
_Avoid_: Section, skill, round

**Run**:
One sitting of a paper, saved on the device until it is handed in.
_Avoid_: Attempt, session

**Score report**:
The 成绩单 of one result of a test (the best one when opened from the book page, the one just handed in when opened from the result page): total, seal and each part's score, printable or saved as a picture.
_Avoid_: Result page, certificate
